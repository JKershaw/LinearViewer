The road to cheap models, reliably

One goal: run a range of model setups per task — Opus for research & review, cheaper models for everything else — and trust the result. That needs four things to land: the account foundation that unlocks presets, the OpenRouter plumbing that bills cheap models to your key, the stepper that lets a smart model supervise a cheap one, and the observability to see it working — transcripts, token spend, wall-clock.
