# V1 Passage debrief: the readers' notes (10 Oct 2026)

The V1 Passage (LIN-3099) ran from 26 Sep to 10 Oct 2026. It made port with V1: one person, one task, one proven merge, on a repo Harbour had never seen. Its debrief is a living document that John and the Flight Companion are still discussing, and it will be added here once it's settled.

These notes are what the debrief was built from. Each is the full report of an independent, read-only reader that the Flight Companion sent out on 10 Oct. They read `origin/main` at `ce3c4ee8` and Harbour's API (reads only). Quotes are verbatim with their source, and anything marked "inference" is the reader's own reading. They are kept as written, so the claims they check refer to the debrief's earlier draft; the corrections they found have been applied there.
