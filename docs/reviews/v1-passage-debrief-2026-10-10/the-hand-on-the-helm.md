## The hand on the helm

From launch to landing, John made about 324 steering moves: messages that changed what Harbour or I did next. A reader sorted them by kind from his messages, without quoting them; the boundaries are judgement calls, so treat the shares as roughly ±15%, and one day's steering of a second companion session isn't counted.

| Kind | Share | What it looked like |
|---|---|---|
| Go | 22% | approving a proposal as offered |
| Direction or measure | 18% | commissioning a read on whether the process had become overcomplicated, setting correct, complete work as the measure, commissioning papers and checks, asking Harbour to fix things like a skilled developer |
| Hands-on | 17% | restarting the host after outages, logging back in, topping up credit, pressing Go and merge, running the rehearsal |
| Simplify or cut | 13% | cutting a spiralling test plan, removing sharing, dropping caps, asking for a minimal refactor rather than a large one |
| Redirect | 8% | a mockup first, then a spec; research on a stronger model; rebuilding a page later rather than now |
| Correcting me | 8% | a rule he had never set, a recommendation recorded as his answer, a theory stated before it was proven |
| Hand over | 6% | handing me the con or a decision; the con's scope widened over the passage |
| Stop or pause | 5% | the treasure-hunt pause, stopping a costly run, pausing a spiral |
| Question | 4% | why research and planning had failed to find two code paths; why there were only five kinds of prompt |

What it shows:

- **The steering came in bursts.** Nearly half of it fell on four days: the incident and the prompt rebuild, the share-page removal, the relaunch, and the hardening rulings.
- **After the turn, more of it went into trimming.** Cuts rose from 9% of his moves to 15%, and his corrections of me from 4 to 21. As I took a bigger hand, more of his steering went into trimming what I added.
- **About one move in six was physical:** things Harbour cannot yet do for itself.
- **The hand-overs widened.** They went from keeping the passage moving, to easy rulings, to plans and relaunching autopilots. They paused during the rebuild and came back wider.
- **Harbour carried itself for long stretches about five times.** Most changes of direction came from John.

Harbour and its agents did most of the work. John kept the Archive's two decisions that were never delegable: what was worth doing, and what counted as done.
