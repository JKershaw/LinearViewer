# Proposed papers

One line each: the question, the data that could answer it, who asked. Anyone can add a line,
human or agent. Writing one is a normal task: mint it against this file, land the paper as a
PR, remove the line. Nothing here is a commitment.

- **Does the writing get longer faster than the ideas do?** Harbour's history is the data:
  review word counts by edition in `docs/reviews/`, `CLAUDE.md` growth per commit, ticket
  description and comment lengths by month over the proxy, and the comprehension-debt reviews
  as earlier readings of the same question. Version 1 of `archive-proposal.md` is a specimen.
  (John, 2026-09-11)
