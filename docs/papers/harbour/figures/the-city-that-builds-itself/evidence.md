The study is observational and covers all known decision sites in scope.

## Two ways to move toward settled

The [17 October Theseus trial table](https://github.com/JKershaw/LinearViewer/blob/68b5d8f3a8c6ee3d4d3dfea87a705d703c7a0192/docs/papers/harbour/consolidate-then-rebuild.md#L45-L62) and its [experiment record](https://github.com/JKershaw/LinearViewer/blob/68b5d8f3a8c6ee3d4d3dfea87a705d703c7a0192/experiments/consolidate-exp3/NOTES.md#L228-L254) cover eight merges, seven method steps and one change to allow more concurrent runs. The six production-change rows with independent behavioural comparisons report zero detected differences on their inputs. The test-only row used a move probe and mutation tests. The concurrent-run change had no replay. A targeted rerun caught a key-order change outside the main sorted comparison. Two formal rulings count responses to ruling cards. John also helped frame the trial.

The [6 November Survey freeze](https://github.com/JKershaw/LinearViewer/blob/68b5d8f3a8c6ee3d4d3dfea87a705d703c7a0192/docs/papers/harbour/cleaner-than-it-started-evidence/2026-11-06-v2/analysis.json) records nine selected area readings across fifteen distinct tickets, all better in both code-only passes. The independent evidence reader recalculated the three tidy-after to parent ratios from recorded API-priced estimates, $6.77 / $14.37 (47%), $7.82 / $11.94 (65%), and $7.18 / $8.41 (85%). These costs exclude later maintenance and human time.

[Runs & Dispatch](https://github.com/JKershaw/LinearViewer/blob/68b5d8f3a8c6ee3d4d3dfea87a705d703c7a0192/docs/plans/survey/area-read-reports/comparison-302edafc3-vs-4b5001ddf.md#L3) and [LLM & Prompts](https://github.com/JKershaw/LinearViewer/blob/68b5d8f3a8c6ee3d4d3dfea87a705d703c7a0192/docs/plans/survey/area-read-reports/comparison-49776653d-vs-e8e92d50a.md#L3) report control-adjusted code-score differences of +0.25 and −0.30.
