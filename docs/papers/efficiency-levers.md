Five levers I noticed with no document of their own. The per-launch workspace `git fetch`
and clone is bounded by `WORKSPACE_FETCH_TIMEOUT_S` but never priced. The retention bounds
that decide whether a resume is even possible (`FOLLOWUP_HOLD_MS`, `REAP_INACTIVITY_MS`)
exist as config constants; `review-loops.md` is the first document to read them as a lever.
Heartbeat post cadence (`HEARTBEAT_POST_CAP_MS`) is tuned with no efficiency argument
attached. Sessions per delivered ticket — 3.8, measured once — has no owning lever. And
nothing argues the non-macOS terminal drivers as a throughput purchase.

What this map cannot tell you. Rows overlap: most intra-session numbers slice the same
carried tokens and must never be summed. Almost every figure comes from one day
(2026-08-14), one run (2026-08-23) or one 30-day window, and several were measured before
the `[1m]` pricing fix, so they are not commensurable with each other. Nothing here says a
lever's saving survives its gate, and nothing is ranked.
