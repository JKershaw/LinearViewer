# Walk 6: what the round 6 design predicts, written before looking

*Written 17 Nov, before the round 6 model is built or any store is carried over. [my reading]*

1. **Same words, two forms.** With each stage's own words in its run, the store reader answers at least as many of the six points' 36 questions right as the ticket reader did (27), and gains most on counts and on what is open, which it can now read off the words and check against the records. It still misses some "addressed to you" items, because walk 5's stores were written when decisions and verdicts couldn't carry a `to`; the previous stage's account in the brief recovers part of that.
2. **Stale is quiet.** On the carried stores, only verdicts and decisions resting on a moved version show stale: the two verdicts at each task's end, and no chains.
3. **Across a lane, the store reader is clearly ahead:** on what is open across the siblings, which asks went unanswered, which decisions were overridden, and the count of waiting entries, against a reader who must reconcile a parent ticket of 100,000+ characters and an area notes file.
4. **The store reader reads far less** at the lane points: its brief plus what it looks up, against the whole parent ticket and notes file.
