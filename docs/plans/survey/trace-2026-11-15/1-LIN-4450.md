## What I notice

*All [my reading], from this one task. Nothing is named as a kind yet.*

- **One finding, retold six times.** The bulk-path gap appears in the place survey, the leftovers, the new note, the blind read, the close-out and the PR. It ends as a note entry with no owner and no end.
- **The live probe moved instead of being done.** The task survey proposed 15 minutes on a real Jira before coding. That became "not verified", then "not run", a PR comment, a piece of its own, two runbooks and a hand-back to John. It needed something no session had: access to John's Jira.
- **The plan lives on the parent and is never changed.** Corrections pile up beside it as comments, and each child carries a slice of it plus pointers. Each of the seven sessions starts from that pile; the start-up figures above suggest about 60,000 to 100,000 tokens each.
- **Text sealed as the person's words is an agent's.** The child's "As asked" block is the breakdown stage's slice of the plan.
- **Rules generate work and text.** The `Retires:` rule pulled an unrelated sweep into the PR. That sweep left three small faults, which were fixed after approval, unreviewed.
- **The person's calls are made by standing text.** The size was "decided by the standing policy". "Settle engineering questions yourself" is in a ticket body, not in code [reported: log:3951].
- **Information leaves the tracker.** The pre-prune description is in Harbour's snapshots, the lessons are in the repository, and the costs are in Harbour's records.

## Why, where I could follow it

- **Finds go to notes because a prompt line says so.** `LEFTOVER_RULE`: "Inside the area but larger: a `## Waiting` entry in that area's `docs/areas/<slug>.md`. Outside the area: a `## Waiting` entry in its note, or a ticket the consequence test sends" (`lib/prompts/leftover-words.js:41`). And `CONSEQUENCE_TEST` sends a find to a ticket only if it breaks something, blocks work or touches safety (`:58`) [checked]. It came in on 12 Nov with LIN-4401 and LIN-4431 (`beyond-the-code.md:85`) [reported].
- **Every build retires a waiting entry because the leftover answer must name one, or give a reason from a closed list.** See `RETIRES_LABEL` and `RETIRES_NONE` (`lib/prompts/leftover-words.js:80-89`) [checked]. That is the LIN-4414 conveyor, one retirement per build (log:3908) [reported].
- **The size goes ahead without the person because of the built-in policy:** "err larger, John's answer of 7 Nov" (`lib/size-of-move.js:12`) [checked].
- **Changes after approval go unreviewed by design.** The close-out template says: "Nobody reviews what you change after the approval" (`lib/prompt-template-defs.js:833` on main) [checked; found while checking trace 4].
