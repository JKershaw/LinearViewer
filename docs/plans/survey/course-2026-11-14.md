# The course to open for business (proposed 14 November 2026)

A proposal for John to rule on. Nothing here is decided. It was plotted from five read-only sub-agent readings (decisions already made, capacity, the distance, structure, method), each citing the survey log, the papers and the review reports. Their working files are in the survey session's scratchpad, not the repository, because one of them lists open safety findings in detail.

## What the readings changed

- **The distance is larger than I said.** The tracker holds 1,366 open tickets. LIN-4400 confirmed a problem on about 700 of them: 134 are unsafe or blocking, and about 570 are broken but not blocking. The notes add 118 Waiting entries (40 defects, 56 copies or cleanups). About 33 review findings sit in neither a ticket nor a note. My "about 250" was the 134 plus the notes: the near list, not the whole one.
- **Most of the budget does not reach the work.** At this week's rate, 80% of the weekly limit ($2,800) bought about 88 landings, at $32 each all-in. Tracked task sessions were about 41% of the burn. The rest went on lane parents, area reads, Periodicals, papers and the supervising session. Only 44 of 115 landings were product or code work.
- **The repository already holds a rebuild method that worked.** Design-first rebuilds of up to 2,816 lines merged in one to two and a half hours with no regression that mattered. They moved no decisions unless the decision was gathered into one owner first. Clean-room rebuilds were unreliable.
- **"Open for business" has no definition or date in the record.** The first door John chose is a small outside team on Jira, using their own runner.
- **No usage evidence exists for any feature, and no area has a written purpose.**

## What I propose "open for business" means

In order, each a door:

1. **Door 1.** The outside Jira team shares one workspace, hands Harbour real tasks, and runs them on their own runner.
2. **V1.** A stranger goes from sign-in, to connected, to a first verified merge on their own logins and model key, without talking to John. This is the north star's own bar. The codebase passes the five-property exam (below).
3. **Later, separate.** Hosted accounts, billing and a free tier. There is no payments code today.

## The method: every trick the record supports

Ranked by size of effect.

1. **Spend on the product.** No new loop steps, no papers, no area reads. One set of Periodicals per gate, as outside readers. This alone moves product landings from about 34 a week to about 100.
2. **Cut before fixing.** Deleting a feature removes its code, tests, notes and safety items at almost no cost. Seven experimental views are about 18,500 source lines and 27,000 test lines.
3. **The lightest loop that the evidence supports, chosen by the paths a change touches.**
   - Small fix: build, one independent review that runs the result, merge.
   - Ordinary task: a ticket that states the behaviour and points at the files; build; one review; a close-out that merges and checks it live.
   - Large move: a map first; John sizes it; one plan and one plan review at the parent; pieces that each delete what they replace.
   - Dropped, on John's ruling: the plan-review loop past one round, the mutation check, close-out filing of follow-ups, the cut rule, and prose notes. Look-first and the blind read have no quality evidence on ordinary tasks and cost 23% of a task.
4. **Shape each task as one decision with the behaviour settled.** Group the known list by file, so one landing settles several items.
5. **Rule in batches, on choices.** Both tidy lanes idled overnight on rulings, and about a third of withdrawn rulings were calls the run could settle. John rules purpose, cut lists and sizes at the start of a phase.
6. **Lanes by file ownership.** Four lanes ran with one conflict. Money binds before parallelism does: four lanes burn the week's 80% in about 42 hours.
7. **Supervision by code.** Harbour runs the lanes. The survey session checks in at gates only.
8. **Already on:** default models per stage, parallel CI. **Untried:** a cheaper judging model, estimated at half again the capacity.

## Phases and gates

Gates buy evidence. Each is read from the code, a rehearsal or a review, and reported.

| Phase | When (budget weeks, reset Thursdays) | What happens | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Set the course | now to 19 Nov | John's rulings: the meaning of open, the cut list, the lighter loop, where the seven safety findings go, a purpose line per area. The Jira live check. | The asks are written and approved; the known list is in one visible place. |
| 1. Clear the deck | week 1 | Cuts. Safety by theme, moving limits from prompt words into code. Stuck states. The two write lanes become one. | No unsafe or blocking item is open without an owner. New work left per landing is under one. |
| 2. Door 1 | weeks 2 to 3 | Shared team workspace. Jira end to end. The day-one path. A fresh-account rehearsal, which has never been run. | A fresh account reaches a verified merge without John. The outside team runs a real task. |
| 3. Shape | weeks 2 to 6, in its own lanes | Large moves in order: write pipeline, `server.js`, providers, browser modules, then the Runs and LLM core. Each gathers the decision, frees the seam with behaviour tests, rebuilds design-first, and ends in deletion. | The one High knot is gone. Seams and Drift read each area as rebuildable. |
| 4. Open | weeks 5 to 8 | A runner a stranger can install with their own credentials. Polish and accessibility. Maintenance mode. The long tail, at rate. | The V1 exam, and a stranger completes the path. |

**The V1 exam.** Every part states its purpose and is checked at its edge; any part is rebuildable from those; one home per rule; nothing unowned; one visible list of known work.

## The arithmetic

All of it is derived or assumed, and the first full week replaces it with a measurement.

- Capacity: about 100 to 115 product landings a week, if 85% of $2,800 reaches product work at $21 to $24 a landing.
- Distance to Door 1: roughly 130 to 180 landings (must-fix items grouped by theme, the missing capabilities, the day-one path). About two weeks.
- Distance to V1: roughly 600 landings including the large moves and half the long tail. About six to eight weeks, so January.
- Stretch 1 is the four weeks to 17 December and should end with Door 1 open and two large moves done. Stretch 2 runs to mid-January and ends at the V1 exam.

## What could sink it

- The 570 non-blocking tickets are sized by guess. Cuts and rebuilds should retire many; nobody has counted how many.
- Dropping look-first and the blind read has no measured quality cost, in either direction.
- Rebuilds above 2,816 lines are untried, and the Runs and LLM core is about 73,000.
- The outside team's needs are known only second-hand.
- Half the plan's speed comes from John ruling early and in batches.
